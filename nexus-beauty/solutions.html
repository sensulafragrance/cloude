Get the best results

Before you start

Routine questions

How long before I see results?

Brightening and acne routines usually show changes in 4–8 weeks. Hair fall routines take 8–12 weeks because hair grows slowly. Use the routine every day and wear sunscreen every morning.

Can I use two kits together?

Yes, but start one at a time. Add the second after 2 weeks so you know how your skin reacts. Use only one exfoliating product and one strong serum per night.

Do I have to buy the whole kit?

No. Untick anything you already own. You get 15% off whenever you choose 3 or more products from a kit.

Are these routines medical advice?

No. They are general routines built from products that sell well in Pakistan. For severe acne, sudden hair loss or melasma, please see a dermatologist.
