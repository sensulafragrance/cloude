Make it safe and easy for anyone in Pakistan to buy beauty products they can trust.

The trend tryer

18–28, follows TikTok and Korean skincare. Wants PDRN, sun sticks and new mists early, and likes a mini before the full size.

02 · Logo

The Nexus mark

Two rings meet, and the berry lens is where they overlap: local and global, science and beauty, store and customer. The wordmark is set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold with "BEAUTY" in spaced Figtree capitals.

Primary lockup. Plum mark and wordmark with a berry lens. Use it on white or blush.

Reversed. White with a pink lens (#E9B8CB) on plum. Use it for the footer, parcel tape and night-time posts.

One colour. All white on berry, or all plum for single-colour printing like stamps and receipts.

Mark and icon. Use the mark alone for the favicon, Instagram profile photo, app icon and product seals.

Clear space

Keep space equal to the height of the "N" in Nexus on every side. Nothing else goes inside that space: no text, no stickers, no image edges.

Minimum size

Below these sizes, "BEAUTY" becomes hard to read. Use the mark on its own instead.

Never do this

Don't stretch or squash the logo. Always scale it evenly.

Don't recolour it outside the brand palette.

Don't place it on busy photos or gradients where it loses contrast.

Don't retype the name in another font or in capitals. Use the logo files.

05 · Signature element

The batch stamp

The dashed batch stamp is the most recognisable Nexus element after the logo. It turns our main promise into something you can see. Use it wherever we make a claim about authenticity.

✓ Verified originalBATCH  AX2609KMFG    03/2026EXP    02/2029

How to build it

  • 1.5 px dashed Plum border, 6 px corners
  • Heading in Figtree 700 caps, Sage, with a tick
  • Codes in IBM Plex Mono, aligned in columns
  • Tilt −3° only on marketing. Keep it straight on product pages and invoices.

Where it appears

  • Hero images and ads
  • Every product page (real codes)
  • The parcel sticker and invoice
  • Unboxing videos and stories

Rule: in customer-facing places (product pages, stickers, invoices) the stamp must show the product's real batch and expiry. Sample codes are only for ads and mock-ups.

06 · Voice & tone

How we talk

We sound like a friend who knows skincare: clear, warm and honest. We explain, we don't hype. Short sentences. Real numbers. No pressure.

Clear

Say what it does and who it's for, in words a first-time buyer understands.

Honest

Give real timelines ("4–6 weeks") and say when a product isn't right for someone.

Warm

Friendly, never cheesy. A light "Assalam o Alaikum" or "Shukriya" in chats is welcome.

Proof-first

Lead with the batch, the source, the size and the price per ml. Let the facts sell.

07 · Claims & trust rules

What we can and can't say

Trust is the whole brand. One fake product or one false claim costs more than any sale earns. These rules apply to everyone who writes, posts or sells for Nexus Beauty.

Always

  • Buy only from the brand or its authorised distributor, and keep the invoice for every batch.
  • Show the real batch number and expiry date on product pages and in the parcel.
  • Use "brightening", "even tone" or "fades dark spots" for pigmentation products.
  • Give sources for numbers ("in a 4-week study of 30 people").
  • Check that own-label products and imports meet DRAP rules before listing.

Never

  • Sell or promote whitening, fairness or skin-bleaching creams, or products without a full ingredient list.
  • Call ourselves an "official" or "authorised" seller unless the brand has confirmed it in writing.
  • Show "before and after" photos that have been edited, filtered or lightened.
  • Write or pay for fake reviews, or remove honest negative ones.
  • Promise medical results: "cures acne", "stops hair fall permanently".
08 · Photography & imagery

Real skin, real light

Our photos should look like a bright, clean bathroom shelf in a Pakistani home, not a studio in Seoul.

People

  • Pakistani models across every skin tone, including deep brown
  • Visible texture: pores, freckles, a little shine
  • Mix of ages, hijab and no hijab, men for men's lines
  • Never lighten skin in editing

Products

  • White or blush background, soft daylight from one side
  • Props from the subject's world: lawn fabric, marble, brass, jasmine
  • Show the batch code on the box in at least one shot
  • Same angle and shadow for every product card

Video

  • Unboxing that shows the batch stamp and sticker
  • Texture close-ups: swatch, spray, pump
  • Real customers and our advisors, subtitled in English and Roman Urdu
  • Vertical 9:16, first 2 seconds show the product
