Description

Vanilla Pistachio opens with roasted pistachio and juicy pear, softens into vanilla orchid and almond milk, and settles on white musk and sandalwood. It smells sweet without being sugary.

Spray it on your hair, neck and clothes. Hair and fabric hold scent longer than skin, so the mist lasts well into the evening.

How to use

1. Hold the bottle about 20 cm away.
2. Spray 2–3 times over the lengths of your hair, not the roots.
3. Add a spray to your neck, wrists and clothes.
4. Top up during the day as you like.

Questions & answers

Questions about this mist

Is it safe to spray on my hair?

Yes. The formula has a low level of alcohol plus panthenol, so it scents hair without drying it out. Spray from about 20 cm onto the lengths, not the roots.

Does it contain alcohol?

Yes, a low level of cosmetic-grade alcohol. It helps the mist dry quickly and spread the scent evenly.

How long does the scent last?

About 3–4 hours on skin and 6–8 hours on hair and clothes, because fibres hold scent longer than skin does.

How is this different from a body spray?

A body spray has a lighter scent that fades within an hour or two. Koh-e-Noor uses more fragrance oil, so it lasts longer and smells richer.

Can I spray it on clothes?

Yes, most fabrics are fine. Test on a hidden spot first on silk and very light colours.
